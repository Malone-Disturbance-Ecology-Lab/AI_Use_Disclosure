AI Use Disclosure Builder

Answer a short survey about how you and AI tools contributed to your paper or project. You'll get a clear, specific disclosure statement that separates your work from the AI's and says what was and wasn't verified.

Nothing is saved. Your answers stay in this browser tab only, with no accounts, cookies, local storage, or analytics. Closing the tab or pressing Erase everything deletes them. If you use the optional chatbot, your answers go only to the AI provider you choose.
